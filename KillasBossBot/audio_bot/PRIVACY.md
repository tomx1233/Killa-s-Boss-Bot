# KILLA'S BOSS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

This explains what **KILLA'S BOSS** ("the bot") collects, why, and how it's handled. The bot processes as little data as possible and stores almost nothing.

## 1. Data we collect

**While you use commands**, the bot may process:
- Your **Discord user ID and username** — used to log which user ran which command and to deliver replies/DMs.
- The **content you provide to commands** — e.g. Roblox asset IDs, uploaded audio files, artists to watch. Uploaded files are read only to analyze them.
- A record of **command usage** in a local log file (`usage.log`), formatted as `[date] user Name (id) used /command ...`.

## 2. Data we store

- **Command usage log** (`usage.log`) — a local file kept on the machine that runs the bot, used for debugging and abuse checks.
- **Configuration** (Discord token, Roblox session cookie) — kept in a local `.env` file on the bot's machine. Never shared, never printed by the bot.

**We do not store your audio.** Files you upload, download, or that the bot fetches are processed in temporary folders that are deleted immediately after each command finishes. Nothing is kept on the bot's machine permanently.

## 3. Why we collect it
The usage log helps the owner fix bugs, prevent abuse, and understand which commands are used. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or selling.

## 4. What we share
The bot sends data only to the services it needs to work:

- **Discord** — to receive your commands and post replies, DMs, and embeds.
- **Roblox** — to check moderation state and download audio on your behalf.

It does not sell or rent your data, and does not share it with any other third party.

## 5. Retention
The usage log is kept while the bot runs and may be reviewed or deleted by the owner. You can request deletion of your logged entries at any time (see Contact below). No audio or uploads are retained.

## 6. Your rights
You can stop using the bot at any time and request that your logged usage be removed. You can also uninstall/remove the bot from your server.

## 7. Contact
To ask what data is held about you or to request deletion, contact the bot owner **K123Killa**. You can reach them on Discord: join their server at **https://discord.gg/AWFAFSCbXQ**.
